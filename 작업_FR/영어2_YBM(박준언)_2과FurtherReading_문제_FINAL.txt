QUESTION:
다음 글의 주제로 가장 적절한 것은?
PASSAGE:
A long-term study conducted by Thomas Gilovich, a psychology professor at Cornell University, reached a powerful and clear conclusion: Don’t spend your money on things. We assume that the happiness we get from buying something will last as long as the thing itself. But it’s wrong. The trouble with things is that the happiness they provide fades quickly. There are three critical reasons for this. First, we get used to new possessions. What once seemed novel and exciting quickly becomes the norm. Second, we keep raising the bar. New purchases lead to new expectations. As soon as we get used to a new possession, we look for an even better one. Third, possessions stimulate humans to compare themselves with others. We buy a new car and are thrilled with it until a friend buys a better one, and there’s always someone with a better one.
CHOICES:
① the essential role of shopping in making people happy
② why familiar possessions bring more joy than novel ones
③ reasons why the pleasure of owning things wears off so fast
④ critical differences between buying cars and buying clothes
⑤ the obvious benefits of comparing ourselves with others
ANSWER:
3
EVIDENCE:
The trouble with things is that the happiness they provide fades quickly. There are three critical reasons for this.
EXPLANATION:
물건에 돈을 쓰지 말라는 연구 결론을 소개한 뒤, 물건이 주는 행복은 빨리 사라진다고 하고 그 세 가지 결정적인 이유(새 물건에 익숙해짐, 계속 높아지는 기준, 남과의 비교)를 설명한다. 따라서 ③이 주제다.
WRONG_ANALYSIS:
① 물건에 돈을 쓰지 말라는 결론이므로 쇼핑이 행복에 필수적이라는 것은 반대 내용이다.
② 새 물건도 곧 익숙해져 평범해진다고 했을 뿐, 익숙한 물건이 더 큰 기쁨을 준다는 내용은 없다.
④ 옷은 언급되지 않으며, 차 구매와 옷 구매의 차이를 다루지 않는다. 차는 남과 비교하는 예로 나올 뿐이다.
⑤ 남과의 비교는 행복을 빨리 사라지게 하는 이유로 제시되었으므로 이점이 아니다.
CHOICES_KR:
① 사람들을 행복하게 하는 데 있어 쇼핑의 필수적인 역할
② 익숙한 소유물이 새로운 것보다 더 큰 기쁨을 주는 이유
③ 물건을 소유하는 즐거움이 그렇게 빨리 사라지는 이유
④ 차를 사는 것과 옷을 사는 것의 결정적인 차이
⑤ 자신을 다른 사람과 비교하는 것의 명백한 이점

QUESTION:

PASSAGE:
First, we get used to new possessions. What once seemed novel and exciting quickly becomes the norm. Second, we keep raising the bar. New purchases lead to new expectations. Gilovich and other researchers have found that experiences deliver longer lasting happiness than things. That’s because experiences become a part of our identity. CHOICES:
① Physical items are the best way to show who we really are.
② Buying a better car than our friends’ brings us enduring joy.
③ Material goods and experiences give us the same kind of happiness.
④ Our experiences remain separate from our sense of self.
